# Research flywheel blueprint

**First publishable:** AI Citation Index 2026  
**Feeds:** DPO (Citation Velocity, Research Influence), DAO (citation accuracy methods)

## Mission

Quarterly original indices that become backlinks, SEO, and AI citations — then DPO measures the authority those reports create (flywheel).

## Two existence features

| Feature | Novel signal | Proof |
|---------|--------------|-------|
| **R1 AI Citation Index 2026** | Industry-facing citation velocity methodology | Dataset + methodology doc under `research/ai-citation-index-2026/` (scaffold) |
| **R2 Open Source Visibility Report** | OSS authority beyond star counts | Outline tied to GitHub collector + home hubs |

## Five questions

Reports must answer what exists in the citation landscape, what changed quarter-over-quarter, why, what will happen if trends continue, and what engineering teams should do.
