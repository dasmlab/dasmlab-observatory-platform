# ADR-0500 — DAO — AI Observatory (Stub)

**Status:** Accepted (MVP blueprint)  
**Date:** 2026-08-07  
**Depends on:** ADR-0001, 0002, 0006, 9999

## Scope

LLM usage, prompt analytics, model governance, inference cost, AI trust—distinct from DPO’s *external* AI visibility (GEO). DAO observes **your** AI systems; DPO observes **how AI systems talk about you**.

## Novel metrics (target)

Model Drift, Prompt Effectiveness, Knowledge Freshness, Citation Accuracy, Reasoning Stability, Inference Cost, AI Trust Score.

## Shared with DPO

Prompt execution plumbing and AI SDK may be shared; product scores and tenants differ.
